Performance Assessment Philosophy

A quick read before you begin — it makes filling your assessment much easier.

Objective of this assessment

The objective is to establish a fair, objective, and evidence-based performance assessment framework for FarmwiseAI — moving away from activity-based assessment and replacing it with outcome-based, impact-based, and adoption-based evaluation.

Performance will not be measured by how many meetings were attended, emails sent, reports prepared, modules written, or experiments conducted. It will be measured by the value created.

In simple terms: we'd love to hear not just what you worked on, but the difference it made.

What This Assessment Asks

Every contribution should answer:

  • What did you create?
  • Was it adopted or used?
  • Who is using it today?
  • What changed because of your contribution?
  • Did it save time, reduce cost, improve quality, increase revenue, strengthen customer confidence, or create a new company capability?
  • How is FarmwiseAI better because of your work?

The purpose is not to reward activity, effort, or intent alone — it is to recognise meaningful contribution, measurable impact, ownership, innovation, adoption, and professional conduct. Performance is measured through:

  • Impact
  • Adoption
  • Ownership
  • Innovation
  • Revenue
  • Cost Reduction
  • Time Reduction
  • Customer Success
  • Team Success
  • Organizational Growth
  • Professional Conduct

Activities vs Outcomes

Less helpful"I attended 20 meetings." · "I wrote 10 reports." · "I developed 5 modules." · "I participated in 15 tenders."
More helpfulRevenue created or influenced · time saved · cost saved · technology adopted · capability created · customer, team & organizational impact.

The Most Important Formula

Impact = Adoption × BenefitIf Adoption = 0, Impact = 0. A brilliant idea nobody uses creates almost no value. A simple idea everyone uses may create enormous value.

Something you build is a great start; what really brings it to life is seeing it adopted, validated, and put to use. That's the moment effort turns into company value — so do share where your work is being used.

Hierarchy of Evidence

Impact is not only revenue or cost. Many roles create value indirectly. Use the strongest evidence you have — any of these is valid.

Level 1 · Strongest

Direct Business Impact

Revenue generated/influenced, cost saved, time saved, headcount avoided, client/project won.
e.g. Helped secure a ₹5 Cr project · Reduced cloud cost by ₹10L/yr · Cut delivery from 6 months to 20 days.
Level 2

Adoption Impact

Did the organization actually use what you built?
Tool adopted by 5 projects · used by 20 engineers · workflow became SOP · model in production.
Level 3

Capability Impact

Did this create a capability the company did not previously possess?
First OCR engine · first satellite monitoring workflow · first drone volume computation system · first AI voice advisory platform · first AI-based land acquisition workflow.
Level 4

Strategic Impact

Did it strengthen the company's position?
Enabled entry into Telangana / Mines Dept · demo used in 10 presentations · new product offering · credibility with government departments.
Level 5

Customer Impact

Did the customer benefit?
Fewer complaints · higher satisfaction · fewer delays · better uptime · improved accuracy.
Level 6 · Often ignored

Team Multiplication Impact

Did your work make other people more productive?
Automation used by 15 employees · reusable library · docs that cut onboarding time · mentoring.

Culture & Integrity Gate

We believe a 50-person, high-trust team can outperform a 100-person team weighed down by politics — so how we work together matters as much as what we deliver. Where there are serious concerns around confidentiality, internal politics, gossip, misrepresentation of work, repeated attendance issues, accountability, respect, or policy, the rating is gently capped one band: Exceptional → Exceeds · Exceeds → Meets · Meets → Below.
